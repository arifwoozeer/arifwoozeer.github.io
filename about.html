About

Mauritius is my parents' home, but it's also the reason simple questions about where I'm from have never really had simple answers.

Grew up in Singapore speaking French and Mandarin, with "MAURITIAN" listed as my race on my NRIC. A lot of childhood was spent translating between languages and explaining myself to people who weren't quite sure where I fit.

During national service in the police force, attended incidents involving people in acute distress, some of them mental health related. That raised questions about what had happened before getting there, and whether there were things that could have been done differently.

At the Reach Alliance, researched multilingual conversational AI for migrant workers' financial literacy. At Over The Rainbow, an Affective Computing capstone became OTR Copilot, an AI-augmented tool for peer mentors. Later, as a peer listener, more direct conversations with people about their problems led to a better sense of where AI systems might be useful in that setting, and where they aren't.

Also co-founded Kura, a journaling app for young people. Five years, S$50K+ in pre-seed funding, around 2,500 users across Singapore, Indonesia and Malaysia, and pilots with NUS and Duke-NUS, before sun-setting it.

These days, work centres on trade and economic relations between Southeast Asia and the Americas, with a surprising amount of time spent on things like why the gap between Brazilian and US interest rates moves capital the way it does.

Outside work, read across economics and philosophy — currently Nāgārjuna and structural gravity models. Working towards DELF B2 in French, picking up Portuguese slowly, and running when possible.
