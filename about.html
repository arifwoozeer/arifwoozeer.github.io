About

Mauritius is my parents' home, but it's also the reason simple questions about where I'm from have never really had simple answers.

Grew up in Singapore speaking French and Mandarin, with "MAURITIAN" listed as my race on my NRIC. A lot of childhood was spent translating between languages and explaining myself to people who weren't quite sure where I fit.

I first got involved with Over The Rainbow as a volunteer peer listener. I spent a lot of time listening to people talk through things they were struggling with, which eventually led to an Affective Computing capstone with the organisation. That project became OTR Copilot, an AI-augmented tool for peer mentors.

I later joined the Reach Alliance, where I researched multilingual conversational AI for migrant workers' financial literacy. I kept volunteering as a peer listener alongside the research, which gave me plenty of opportunities to see where technology could be useful in these conversations and where it probably wasn't.

I also co-founded Kura, a journaling app for young people. Five years, S$50K+ in pre-seed funding, around 2,500 users across Singapore, Indonesia and Malaysia, and pilots with Duke-NUS, before we eventually shut it down.

During national service in the police force, I attended incidents involving people in acute distress, some of them mental health related. I found myself thinking about what had happened before we arrived, and whether some of those situations could have gone differently.

These days, I work on trade and economic relations between Southeast Asia and the Americas. Some days that means spending an unreasonable amount of time figuring out why the gap between Brazilian and US interest rates moves capital the way it does.

Outside work, I read across economics and philosophy — currently Nāgārjuna and structural gravity models. Working towards DELF B2 in French, picking up Portuguese slowly, and running when possible.

You can reach me at arifwoozeer@gmail.com or via LinkedIn, GitHub, and Goodreads.
