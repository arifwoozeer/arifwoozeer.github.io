Research

I’m interested in what happens when people bring complex, ambiguous situations to conversational AI, particularly when a model’s instructions come into tension with its learned patterns of response. I study the gap between what a model is instructed to do and how it behaves in practice, with a focus on the interaction between AI systems and human behaviour.

Therapeutic priors in frontier language models

Independent research · 2026–present

This pilot study tests how frontier language models behave in therapeutic-style conversations when their instructions conflict with their existing tendencies. The experiment keeps the system instruction fixed while gradually increasing pressure from the user, testing when a model starts giving advice despite being told not to, and whether models with stronger advice-giving tendencies are more likely to break the instruction.

The study also explores whether chain-of-thought and mechanistic interpretability can help explain these behaviours. The longer-term goal is to develop an experiment that can be repeated across different models as they change.

OTR Copilot

Affective Computing capstone · Over The Rainbow

OTR Copilot is an AI tool for peer mentors at Over The Rainbow. The project explored how AI could support an existing mentoring relationship without becoming part of the relationship itself.

The work was presented as a digital poster at the UCL Behaviour Change Conference and will be presented as an oral presentation at AMIA 2026.

Multilingual conversational AI and migrant worker financial literacy

Reach Alliance · Singapore

The Reach Alliance is a global university consortium founded at the University of Toronto that brings students and researchers together to study complex challenges affecting underserved communities. Its research is interdisciplinary and field-based, with projects conducted in collaboration with local communities and partners.

This project with migrant workers in Singapore examined how language affects financial decision-making when people use conversational AI. The project focused on how migrant workers used these tools across languages and where they ran into problems.

The research was conducted through the Reach Alliance and is documented in a case study.
