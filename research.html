Research

I study how conversational AI responds when people seek support in complex, ambiguous situations, and how it accounts for their circumstances, constraints and relationships.

Emotional support when professional care is difficult to access

Independent research · 2026–present

This study examines what assistance conversational AI offers when professional care is unaffordable, delayed or difficult to use privately. It asks whether suggestions respect a person’s stated circumstances, and what support the model provides for the concern that brought them to the conversation.

The planned experiment uses fictional conversations set in Singapore, varying details such as available money, privacy and willingness to disclose personal information. It examines whether a model adjusts its suggestions when these details become clear, and whether its responses differ when the same information is available from the outset.

The work builds on questions from OTR Copilot about how AI suggestions fit the person receiving support. Its longer-term aim is to inform tools that help human peer supporters recognise what a suggestion assumes and whether it fits what someone has shared.

OTR Copilot

Affective Computing capstone · Over The Rainbow

OTR Copilot is an AI tool for peer mentors at Over The Rainbow. The project explored how AI could support an existing mentoring relationship without becoming part of the relationship itself.

The work was presented as a digital poster at the UCL Behaviour Change Conference and will be presented as an oral presentation at AMIA 2026.

Multilingual conversational AI and migrant worker financial literacy

Reach Alliance · Singapore

The Reach Alliance is a global university consortium founded at the University of Toronto that brings students and researchers together to study complex challenges affecting underserved communities. Its research is interdisciplinary and field-based, with projects conducted in collaboration with local communities and partners.

This project examined how migrant workers in Singapore used conversational AI across languages, where they ran into problems, and how language affected their financial decision-making. The work is documented in a case study.
